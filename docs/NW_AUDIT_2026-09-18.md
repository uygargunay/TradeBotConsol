# NW logic review — 18 September 2026

Reviewed base: `a02b661` (`origin/master`, merged NW-only / multi-timeframe work).
This patch fixes execution and data defects. It does not establish a profitable NW profile.
The checked-in strategy thresholds and selected timeframes are unchanged.

## Confirmed defects addressed

| Priority | Finding | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | Native IBKR hourly candles and the bot's 09:30-anchored live candles had different boundaries. Relabeling native candles could overwrite the opening sub-bar and distort NW. | Build 30m, 1h and 4h history from aligned 30m source bars; use 15m sources for 15m. Stage responses, reject obsolete requests, and publish only at history completion. |
| High | Partial fills were ignored until `Filled`, and `execDetails` was ignored entirely. | Accumulate changes in cumulative quantity and notional from both callbacks, deduplicate repeats, and charge the modeled commission once per order. |
| High | A local exit could be sent before its native stop was confirmed cancelled. A stop fill in that interval could produce a second sell. | Defer the exit until protection is cancelled; account for intervening fills and resize the exit. Error 202 alone cannot release it because it contains no fill quantity. |
| High | An entry timeout or day reset could discard a working entry's tracking. Submission exceptions were treated as proof that no order existed. | Preserve reservations through cancellation, persist working-order fill state and pending NW audit metadata, and reconcile uncertain submissions. |
| High | Changing the NW stop percentage did not reliably change an existing position's stop; local NW exits also depended on unrelated minute-history readiness. | Calculate NW stops from the current setting and average fill; synchronize native GTC stops, including remaining quantity after partial fills. NW exits no longer require minute-history availability. |
| High | Reconciliation timeout enabled entries despite unknown account state; reconnect did not rebuild subscriptions. | Require both position and open-order snapshots, retry without enabling entries, restore feeds/history on reconnect, and prioritize held symbols even outside the watchlist. |
| Medium | NW sizing inherited Kelly estimates from other, disabled strategies. | Use configured fixed fractional risk and the existing drawdown reduction for NW, without estimating a Kelly edge from the mixed trade history. |
| Medium | Empty/invalid timeframe selections were silently remapped, and legacy 30m settings silently gained additional intervals. | Reject invalid choices; preserve an explicitly saved legacy interval. Validate NW envelope and stop settings before applying them. |

IBKR documents duplicate or absent `orderStatus` callbacks and recommends also monitoring executions: [order submission documentation](https://interactivebrokers.github.io/tws-api/order_submission.html). Its [historical-data lesson](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/campus/trading-lessons/python-receiving-market-data/) also illustrates the shortened first hourly bar and subsequent clock-hour boundaries.

## What the current NW rule actually does

- Uses a causal Gaussian endpoint estimate and mean absolute error from completed regular-session bars. It is not guaranteed to match a chart's repainting NW implementation.
- Arms when a live price is above the fixed lower band, then buys on a fresh downward cross. A stock already below the band at startup is not sufficient.
- Evaluates selected timeframes independently. If multiple cross on the same tick, the longest interval wins attribution; other simultaneous crossings are recorded as confirmations.
- Holds one net position per symbol. Different symbols can trade concurrently. This does not maintain separate same-symbol lots for each timeframe.
- Keeps the configured +5% gross target and 3% protective stop. NW positions are exempt from automatic end-of-day liquidation.
- The profit sell is generated locally. The bot, live feed and connection must be running to react to the target. A disconnected bot has no native GTC profit order to capture a temporary +5% touch.

## Evidence available

The supplied older history has 38 completed trades: 24 scalp trades (net -$79.78), eight Momentum trades (-$42.81), four Gap-and-Go trades (-$18.94), and only two NW trades (+$1.46 combined after modeled commissions). Both NW trades used the old end-of-day exit. This is not a sample of the current multi-day 5% rule.

The screenshot of open positions is not enough to reconstruct the PLTR signal. Establishing why that particular entry occurred requires its entry timestamp, entry-time NW audit record and corresponding bars/ticks. The defects above are plausible sources of discrepancies, not proof of the cause of that trade.

Earlier descriptions of a "proven" profile were too strong. There is no validated performance ranking for 15m, 30m, 1h or 4h in the available evidence, and this repository's backtest is not an end-to-end historical replay of this NW multi-timeframe execution path.

## Settings worth keeping or testing

| Setting | Current value | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NW lookback | 250 bars | Keep as the baseline; there is no evidence to optimize it yet. |
| Bandwidth | 6 bars | Keep. Test 8 separately if desired; more smoothing also adds lag and does not guarantee better entries. |
| Band multiplier | 2.5 | Keep as control. Paper-test 3.0 separately: the lower threshold is farther below the same centerline, so the entry rule is more selective in price, but can still catch a continuing decline. |
| Profit target | 5% | Keep as requested. This is gross price gain before fees and slippage. |
| Protective stop | 3% | Keep while comparing entries. Tightening to 1% is a major strategy change, not an automatic improvement. The settings UI/backend now agree on the broker override's 4% maximum. |
| Timeframes | 30m, 1h, 4h | Retained. For measurement, use 30m as a control and evaluate the others separately; simultaneous portfolio constraints can otherwise obscure their individual results. Leave 15m off until tested. |
| Risk per entry | 0.4% of $4,000 budget | Consider 0.2% with four positions, or retain 0.4% with two positions. This aligns nominal stop exposure more closely with the $40 daily loss threshold; it is not a hard loss cap. |

Four entries at the current 0.4% risk can represent about $64 of planned price loss plus $8 of modeled round-trip commissions. A $40 daily halt blocks new entries; it does not erase that already-open exposure. At 0.2%, the comparable nominal total is approximately $40 including those fees. Whole-share rounding and budget caps reduce some orders further; a lower risk setting can prevent any purchase of higher-priced shares. Gaps and slippage can exceed planned stop losses.

Fees also change the apparent 5:3 payoff. Assuming exactly $2 round-trip fees and fills at the target/stop, a $250 position needs about a 47.5% win rate to break even; a $500 position needs 42.5%. The commission-free figure would be 37.5%. These are arithmetic examples, not forecasts.

The most useful entry experiment is **reclaim confirmation**: observe price below the lower band, then require a move back above it before entry, using a clearly specified completed-bar or live-tick rule. It could avoid some entries during an uninterrupted fall, but may miss fast rebounds or enter at a worse price. It is a proposed comparison, not implemented or proven in this patch.

Change one variable at a time. Compare net expectancy, drawdown, time held, maximum adverse/favorable excursion, and results by timeframe on later data, including spread, fees and overnight gaps. Win rate alone cannot select the better strategy.

## Validation and limits

- .NET 8 compilation and 20 focused regression scenarios passed. Compiler warnings remain.
- Dashboard JavaScript parses; configuration JSON parses; Git whitespace checks pass.
- Regression scenarios cover partial and duplicate fills, execution-only callbacks, commission accounting, stop/exit cancellation races, pending-state serialization, day reset, stop updates, reconciliation barriers, timeframe aggregation and validation.
- No broker connection, orders, live deployment or IBKR paper-session validation was performed.
- Session boundaries still assume normal 09:30–16:00 US equity sessions; there is no exchange holiday/early-close calendar. Do not treat shortened sessions as validated.
- A partially filled bracket parent can leave its child held at IBKR until the parent completes. Local protection and cancellation tracking now account for partial shares, but this is not an independently active native stop for every partial parent fill.
- State uses local files and modeled commissions. It is not a transactional execution ledger, and it does not reconstruct all fills that occurred while the process was stopped. An unknown or mismatched position/order requires review against TWS.
