1. The "Entry" Gates (Must all be TRUE)Time: 
	Must be between 08:10 AM and 12:40 PM PST.
	Broad Market Trend: The QQQ 20-period SMA must be above the 100-period SMA.
	Individual Stock Trend: The specific stock's 20-SMA must be above its 100-SMA.
	Cooldown: The stock must not be in the "Penalty Box" from a previous sell.
2. The "Exit" Logic (Triggers a SELL)Trailing Stop: 
	If the price drops by the specific % (e.g., 2% for AAPL) from its highest point since you bought it.
	Profit Target: If the price hits your goal (e.g., 1.2% for MSFT).
	Hard Stop: If the price drops below the initial entry stop level.
	End of Day: At 12:45 PM PST, all open positions are sold regardless of profit/loss to avoid "overnight gap" risk.
